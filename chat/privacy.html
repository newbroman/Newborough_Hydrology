Privacy: Ask Newborough Warren

The chatbot on the Newborough Warren hydrology site. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Ask Newborough Warren answers questions about the Newborough Warren groundwater study from the study's published report and data. It is run by Martin Hollingham, the study's author, who is responsible for the information described here.

What is recorded

In short: your questions are recorded; you are not.

Each question you ask is recorded, with the answer it received. This is so that wrong or unhelpful answers can be found and fixed, and so the published material can be improved where people's questions show a gap.

Please don't type personal information (names, addresses, contact details) into the box.

Ten questions a day

Each visitor can ask 10 questions a day here; the count resets at midnight (UTC). To count them, the service uses a one-way scrambled form of your IP address (a salted hash) that cannot be turned back into the address. It is held only for counting and deleted after two days, and it is never stored with your questions. People sharing a connection, such as an office network, share the ten.

The Claude version

When your ten are used, the page offers a link to the same assistant inside Claude, Anthropic's own app. That version runs on your own Claude account: you need to be signed in, Claude asks you once to allow it, and your questions count towards your own Claude usage. It starts a new conversation and does not see what you asked here. Questions you ask there go through your Claude account under Anthropic's privacy policy; they are not sent to this site's service and are not in its record of questions.

Feedback

Under each answer you can mark it Helpful or Something's wrong and add a note, and the footer has a Send feedback box for anything else. Feedback is recorded the same way as questions: the note, the time, and which answer it is about, with nothing that identifies you. It is kept for 12 months, and each connection can send a few notes a day. Because it is anonymous we cannot reply; if you want a reply or a discussion, open an issue on the study's GitHub page (this needs a GitHub account, and issues there are public).

Who handles the information

  • Anthropic writes the answers. Your question, and the passages the page found for it, are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the service that passes questions to Anthropic and stores the record of questions. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • GitHub serves the web page itself, as it does the rest of this site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The questions are not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose other than running and improving this service and the study's published material.

How long it is kept

Questions and answers are deleted automatically after 12 months.

Searching without sending anything

When written answers are unavailable, the box searches the published documents inside your browser instead. Nothing you type is sent anywhere in that mode.

Questions about this policy

Open an issue on the study's GitHub page. If you want a question you asked removed, say roughly when you asked it and what it said.
